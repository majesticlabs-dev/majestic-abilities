# Quality Loop

Use this before delivery or publication.

## Review Lenses

### Search Intent

- The title promise is answered.
- The first 300 words give the reader the core answer.
- The content type matches the SERP and reader job.

### Differentiation

- The piece has a specific angle.
- It does not merely summarize competitors.
- It includes information gain.

### Trust

- Material claims are sourced or labeled.
- Limitations are stated where needed.
- Author or brand expertise is visible only when supplied or verifiable.
- Hypothetical examples are labeled, and no experience, opinion, expertise, or specificity is invented.

### Conversion

- CTA matches awareness level.
- The next step is low-friction.
- BOFU pages use proof, not educational filler.

### AEO Readiness

- Definitions are concise.
- Questions have direct answers.
- Entities are clear.
- Source-backed facts are easy to cite.

## Stop Conditions

Do not deliver as publication-ready if:

- claims are unsourced and risky
- experience, expertise, authorial opinion, examples, or specificity were invented
- the topic duplicates the ledger
- the piece has no clear reader job
- the CTA is unrelated to the content
- the draft fails deterministic audits without explanation
