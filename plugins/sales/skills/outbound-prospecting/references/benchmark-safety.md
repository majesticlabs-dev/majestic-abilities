# Outbound Benchmark Safety

Use internal cohorts first. Compare like-for-like segments by list source, buyer, geography, channel, period, and offer.

Prioritize: delivered contacts, replies, positive replies, meetings booked, meetings held, qualified opportunities, and downstream revenue quality. Open rate is directional only because privacy protections and image preloading distort it.

External ranges must include source, publication date, population, metric definition, and known limitations. They are context, not targets. Diagnose the earliest evidenced break, but do not infer copy failure from opens alone.
