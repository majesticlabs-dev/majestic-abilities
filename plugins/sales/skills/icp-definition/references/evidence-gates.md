# ICP Evidence Gates

Apply these gates when turning strengths, weaknesses, and triggers into a target definition. They separate a defensible ICP from a wish list.

## Requires, not prefers

For each fit criterion, ask what customer circumstance requires an extreme version of the product's strength. A circumstance that merely prefers the strength produces a trade-off shopper who picks by spreadsheet. Keep criteria that can drive the purchase by themselves; demote the rest to table stakes.

## Real-segment gate

A criterion survives only when a significant, nameable segment typifies it. Describe segments by circumstance and behavior, not demographics. If no real segment can be named, the criterion is table stakes: record it as a cut, not hopefully.

Concentrate on the best customers: what describes the best customers but not the worst is signal. What describes all customers predicts nothing about buying, paying, or staying.

## Deal-breaker versus friction

For each weakness or disqualifier, ask whether any circumstance makes it a hard no:

- **Deal-breaker:** a circumstance where the product is disqualified no matter how well everything else fits. Name the anti-market segments that live in those circumstances and exclude them deliberately from targeting, features, and marketing.
- **Friction:** costs points in comparisons but disqualifies no one. Record it as backlog input, not a market boundary.

Deal-breakers also sharpen fit criteria: append the qualifiers they force, such as a minimum spend level or an existing platform. Park "we could fix that weakness" ideas as strategy input; the map describes the market the product can win today.

## Trigger evidence status

Mark every buying trigger with one of two states:

- **Observed:** harvested from customer accounts of what prompted the search and why they chose, with its source. Prefer asking shortly after purchase while the answer is fresh.
- **Hypothesized:** worked backward through crisis, seasonal cycle, strategic-window, or life-change lenses. A hypothesis is a guess wearing a plan; name the next conversation that tests it.

An observed trigger outranks a vivid hypothesis. Never dress brainstorming as evidence.

## Findability

For each trigger, state how to find prospects in that condition: the exact phrases they search, the public signals they leave, the communities where they post. A trigger that cannot be detected from outside is messaging-only; annotate that limit rather than dropping the trigger.

## No averaging

When candidate segments diverge, force a choice of primary segment. Averaged criteria describe no one and qualify no one. Record the rejected segments and why.
