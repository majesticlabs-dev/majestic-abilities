# Account Tiering Worksheet

Use tiers to allocate research and sales effort, not to declare account value as fact.

| Field | Question | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fit | Does the account meet explicit ICP criteria? | Public facts, CRM history |
| Trigger | Is there a current reason to act? | Event, behavior, direct statement |
| Access | Can the likely buying group be reached ethically? | Known relationship or channel |
| Value | Can the offer plausibly create relevant value? | Comparable verified outcome |
| Cost | What support, implementation, or risk burden exists? | Delivery evidence |

Define tiers from the decision context. Record unknowns separately and retier when evidence changes. Never infer sensitive traits or fabricate triggers.
