# Banned AI Phrase Candidates

Treat this as a review list, not a universal blacklist. Keep a ban only when it conflicts with the source voice or intended context.

## Generic Openers

- In today's...
- In a world where...
- It's important to note that...
- It's worth noting that...
- When it comes to...
- Let's dive into...
- Let's delve into...

## Mechanical Transitions

- Furthermore
- Additionally
- Moreover
- Moving forward
- At the end of the day
- To put this in perspective
- What makes this particularly interesting is

## Inflated Vocabulary

Review unsupported uses of:

- leverage
- utilize
- unlock
- supercharge
- robust
- seamless
- cutting-edge
- game-changer
- transformative
- holistic

## Engagement Bait

- Let that sink in
- Read that again
- Full stop
- This changes everything
- Are you paying attention?

## Mechanical Contrast Structures

Review these unless the source samples establish them as genuine habits:

- This isn't X. This is Y.
- Not X. Y.
- Forget X. This is Y.
- Less X, more Y.
- It's not about X. It's about Y.

## Vague Authority

- some experts say
- research shows, without naming the research
- studies suggest, without naming the study
- many people believe
- it is widely regarded

## Rule

Replace vague instructions such as "do not sound like AI" with exact phrases, structures, punctuation, or claim patterns that can be checked.
