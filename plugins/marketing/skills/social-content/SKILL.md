# Social Content

## Boundary

Own professional social distribution. Do not present ranking folklore, fixed posting times, or reach multipliers as facts, and do not fabricate personal stories or results.

## Required Inputs

- Platform, account type, audience, and goal
- Expertise, source material, proof, and point of view
- Current platform limits and policy evidence
- Voice, cadence capacity, and desired action

## Workflow

1. Verify current platform format, account, analytics, and policy constraints from official sources.
2. Choose a content pillar and reader payoff linked to the stated goal.
3. Select a single post or thread based on idea complexity, not a preferred length.
4. Draft an explicit opening, evidence-backed body, and proportionate next action.
5. For threads, make each post useful and preserve a coherent arc.
6. Plan a sustainable cadence and substantive response behavior.
7. Measure relevant actions such as qualified replies, saves, sends, clicks, or watch time without claiming hidden weights.

## Output

1. **Platform assumptions and verification date**
2. **Content pillars and cadence**
3. **Complete post or thread drafts**
4. **Measurement and review plan**

## Quality Gate

- Current behavior is verified or labeled unknown.
- The opening does not use a false curiosity gap.
- Claims, stories, and numbers are supplied or sourced.
- Content remains useful outside a hoped-for algorithm boost.
