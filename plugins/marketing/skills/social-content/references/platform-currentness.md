# Platform Currentness Checklist

Before platform-specific advice, verify from official documentation: post and thread limits, account-type differences, analytics definitions, link and media behavior, recommendation policies, automation restrictions, and advertising or disclosure rules. Record the date.

Treat saves, shares, comments, clicks, and watch time as observable analytics, not proof of unpublished ranking weights. Do not repeat fixed reach multipliers, magic posting times, first-hour rules, or link-placement folklore without current authoritative evidence.
