# Content Repurposing

## Boundary

Adapt one source asset. A named source asset is required: drafting channel-native content from a pillar, idea, or cadence plan is out of scope, as are customer-proof derivatives whose quotations or outcomes need approval and publication permission. Do not claim every excerpt fits every channel, create unsupported stories, or reduce repurposing to copy-paste truncation.

## Required Inputs

- Canonical source asset and publication rights
- Audience, goal, channels, and desired formats
- Claims, citations, voice, and prohibited edits
- Channel constraints and available visual or audio material

## Workflow

1. Extract the source thesis, key evidence, examples, quotations, and limitations.
2. Mark elements that can stand alone and those that require context.
3. Map each element to a channel, format, buyer stage, and action.
4. Rewrite for the channel while preserving claim scope, attribution, and source link.
5. Create multiple angles only when the source supports them.
6. Check that derivatives add context, utility, or interaction rather than duplicating text.

## Output

1. **Source-element inventory**
2. **Channel and format map**
3. **Complete derivative drafts**
4. **Attribution and evidence notes**
5. **Publication sequence**

## Quality Gate

- Every derivative traces to the source.
- Quotations and metrics remain accurate.
- Channel adaptation is substantive.
- No algorithm folklore drives the format.
