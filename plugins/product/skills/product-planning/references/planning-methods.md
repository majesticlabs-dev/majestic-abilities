# Planning Methods

RICE = reach × impact × confidence / effort. ICE = impact × confidence × ease. Use ranges when inputs are uncertain and test whether modest input changes reverse the ranking. Scores compare a defined set, not universal value.

An opportunity tree connects one measurable outcome to evidenced customer opportunities, candidate solutions, and tests. Keep opportunities in customer language.

Now, Next, and Later represent descending confidence. Now has owners and credible commitments. Next has dependencies and evidence gaps. Later contains hypotheses, not promises. Every roadmap includes what is not being done and when the decision will be reviewed.
