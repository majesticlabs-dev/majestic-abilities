# Discovery Methods

## Interview prompts

Ask about the last real occurrence: trigger, sequence, workaround, people involved, cost, search, alternatives, decision, and result. Ask what changed, not whether a proposed feature sounds useful. Preserve exact quotes with consent and separate notes from interpretation.

## Forces of progress

Record push from the old situation, pull toward the new solution, anxiety about switching, and habits supporting the status quo. A valid job statement names situation, desired progress, and outcome without embedding a solution.
