# Pricing Research

For every competitor price, record URL, access date, segment, billing period, included usage, discounts, fees, and whether the figure was directly verified. Do not calculate an average across unlike packages.

Willingness-to-pay evidence can include completed purchases, lost-deal reasons, expansion behavior, controlled offer tests, and structured price-sensitivity interviews. Stated intent is weaker than behavior.

Model current, lower, and higher scenarios with ranges for conversion, retention, expansion, support cost, and migration. Show which assumption drives the result and define rollback conditions before rollout.
