# Current Paid Search Checks

Verify in current official documentation before implementation:

- available Search and cross-network campaign types
- exact, phrase, broad, close-variant, and keywordless behavior
- automated bidding prerequisites and which manual adjustments still apply
- conversion, consent, enhanced-conversion, and attribution requirements
- ad format limits, asset requirements, and restricted-content policies

Do not assume a historic budget split, match-type mix, campaign ramp schedule, or industry benchmark remains valid. Record the source and date for material platform claims.
