# Community Rule Verification Checklist

Verify at execution time, per community, and record the date:

- the platform's current terms, spam policy, and self-promotion rules
- the community's own posted rules, sidebar, wiki, and pinned moderator notes
- account age, karma, reputation, or verification thresholds for posting or linking
- link, image, and referral restrictions, including domain blocklists
- required disclosure, flair, tagging, or AMA and vendor-thread conventions
- whether promotion is confined to a scheduled thread or a designated channel

Rules change without notice and differ between communities on the same platform. There is no universal self-promotion ratio. Do not carry a rule from one community to another, rely on a cached or summarized copy, act on a rule verified in a previous session, or treat moderator silence as permission. When a rule is ambiguous, ask a moderator before posting and record the answer.
