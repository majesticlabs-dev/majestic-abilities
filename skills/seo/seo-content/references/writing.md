# Writing Standards

Write for a smart buyer with limited patience.

## First Paragraph Rule

Answer the search query in the first 2-3 sentences. Do not open with market throat-clearing.

Bad:

> In today's rapidly evolving digital landscape, teams are increasingly looking for ways to streamline workflows.

Better:

> AI marketing tools help teams automate repeat research, drafting, routing, and reporting work. The useful ones save hours because they remove a specific manual step, not because they sprinkle AI on a dashboard.

## Specificity Rule

Replace vague claims with concrete detail.

Weak:

> This saves time.

Stronger:

> This cuts the weekly content refresh pass from 4 hours to 45 minutes by turning Search Console exports into ranked update tasks.

## So-What Chain

For every feature, ask why the buyer cares until you reach a business outcome.

```text
Feature -> workflow change -> pain removed -> business result
```

Write from the business result, then show the workflow change.

## Buyer Workflow Rule

For B2B content, explain:

- how the buyer does the job today
- why that workaround persists
- what breaks as volume or risk increases
- why the proposed solution is better
- what data or proof validates that claim

## Evidence Rule

Use one of these for material claims:

- cited source
- first-party data
- customer example
- product screenshot or workflow artifact
- clearly labeled operator experience

If none exists, weaken the claim or cut it.

## Style Rules

- Prefer short paragraphs.
- Prefer contractions.
- Avoid hype.
- Avoid filler transitions.
- Use bullets when the reader is comparing options.
- Use tables when dimensions repeat.
