# Sample Selection Guide

Voice matching depends on representative source material.

## Strong Samples

Use three to five pieces where the same person or organization communicated deliberately:

- essays or newsletters
- blog posts
- memos or proposals
- thoughtful emails
- edited social posts
- product or support writing with a recognizable voice
- before-and-after edits approved by the voice owner

Prefer pre-AI samples. When samples are AI-assisted, identify what the owner materially changed.

## Weak Samples

Exclude or label:

- short replies without enough prose to measure
- raw transcripts
- copied quotations
- model-generated drafts with little human editing
- keyword-stuffed pages
- legal or compliance boilerplate
- work written for a substantially different audience
- mixed-author content with unclear ownership

## Minimum Viable Set

When stronger material is unavailable, request:

1. one longer sample the owner considers representative
2. one shorter sample the owner considers representative
3. one draft the owner rejected or heavily rewrote

Treat findings from a small corpus as provisional.

## Corpus Record

For each source, record:

| Source | Author | Channel | Audience | Approximate date | Provenance | Approval status | Why representative | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| | | | | | | | | Corpus, holdout, excluded, duplicate, or failed |

When collection is involved, record the selection rule, discovered and retained counts, total words, exclusions, exact duplicates, and failures. Keep this provenance outside the prose used for measurement.

Reserve one representative sample as a holdout when enough material remains to build a credible profile. Do not choose the holdout after seeing which sample makes the profile look best.

## Register Check

Before combining samples, ask whether differences reflect:

- audience
- channel
- stakes
- time period
- multiple authors
- intentional repositioning

Do not average distinct authors or incompatible registers into a fictional voice.
